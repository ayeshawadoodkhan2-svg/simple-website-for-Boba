All About Boba Tea

Types of Milk Tea

  • Black Milk Tea: The standard one everyone gets with black tea and milk.
  • Taro Milk Tea: Purple tea that tastes sweet and creamy.
  • Hojicha Milk Tea: Roasted green tea flavor, less sweet.

Fruit Teas

  • Mango Green Tea: Very sweet and fruity, usually comes with green tea.
  • Passionfruit Tea: Sour and refreshing.

Toppings

You can add different things at the bottom of your drink:

  1. Tapioca pearls (boba)
  2. Coconut jelly
  3. Pudding
